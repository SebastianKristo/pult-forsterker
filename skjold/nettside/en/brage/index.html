Skjold Brage

Smart devices without the cloud

Brage is the smart device line from Skjold Lab, next to the audio products. Each device is a small, 3D printed box that talks directly to Home Assistant on your own network. There is no cloud, no account and no separate app.

See Brage Vippe 5Register interest ›

No cloud

Everything runs on your own network. The devices work even when the internet is down.

Home Assistant

The devices are found automatically, with no account and no bridge.

Homey

Use them in Homey through Home Assistant.

Open firmware

The devices run ESPHome. You can read and change everything yourself.

Wireless updates

New firmware is sent over Wi-Fi. You do not need to open the box.

Build it yourself

The case is 3D printed, and the parts are ordinary off-the-shelf parts.

FIRST DEVICE

Brage Vippe 5

Five metal toggle switches in a box that hangs under your desk. Each switch is its own on/off switch in your smart home. The switches stay where you put them, so you always see what is on.

HOW IT WORKS

Flip a switch and something happens at home

Brage Vippe only reports how the switches are set. What should happen is up to you, in Home Assistant or Homey.

1

Connect power

Vippe 5 gets 5 V over USB-C, from a charger or a free USB port.

2

Set up Wi-Fi

The device makes its own network the first time. Choose your home network there.

3

Add it to Home Assistant

It shows up by itself, with five switches: Vippe 1 to Vippe 5.

4

Choose what the switches do

Link each switch to a light, a socket or a scene.

WHAT THE SWITCHES CAN CONTROL

One switch, one thing

Lights

The desk lamp, the ceiling light or the whole room.

Monitor and speakers

Turn on your workspace with a smart plug.

Scenes

One scene when the switch is flipped on, and another when it is flipped off.

Anything Home Assistant can do

The switch is an ordinary sensor, so it works in any automation.

SAFE TO BUILD

Only 3.3 volts in the switches

The switches only carry a weak signal from the circuit board, never mains voltage. What actually turns the power on and off is the smart plugs and lamps you already have.

THE NAMES

Brage, function and count

The devices are named Brage, then the function, then the number of channels. So Brage Vippe 5 has five toggle switches (“vippe” is Norwegian for toggle). The mount under the desk is called Brage Skinne. The name comes from Bragi, the god of poetry in Norse mythology.
