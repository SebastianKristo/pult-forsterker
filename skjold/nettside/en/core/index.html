Skjold Core

Skjold on a plain Raspberry Pi

Core is Skjold with no case, buttons or display. Flash an SD card with Skjold Flasher, connect the Pi to speakers or an amplifier, and control everything from the app, the browser, Home Assistant or Homey. It plays in sync with S1, Node and Touch in other rooms.

SKJOLD CORE

Get started with Core

Skjold on a plain Raspberry Pi 4 or 3B/3B+, with no case, buttons or display.

1

Flash the card

Open Skjold Flasher, choose Core and enter your Wi-Fi.

2

Connect

Put the card in the Pi and connect speakers or an amplifier.

3

Wait for the first boot

The first boot takes 15–30 minutes while Skjold installs.

4

Open Skjold

Go to http://<navn>.local:8765 or use the app.

SOURCES

Sources

AirPlay 2

From iPhone, iPad and Mac. With AirPlay 2, several rooms can play at once.

Bluetooth

From any phone. Start pairing from the app or the browser.

Spotify Connect

Pick Skjold in the Spotify app. The music streams directly, and you can put your phone away.

Internet radio

Up to 50 favourites, with search across Norwegian stations and the whole world right on the screen.

INTEGRATIONS

Integrations with Lyrion, Home Assistant, Homey and Spotify

LYRION MUSIC SERVER

Lyrion

Skjold shows up as a player in Lyrion, with radio, favourites, local files and synchronised groups across rooms. Skjold connects to an existing Lyrion server, or can run the server itself.

HOME ASSISTANT

Home Assistant

Skjold is discovered automatically as a media player with source, volume and on/off. It can be used in automations, for example radio when you get home.

HOMEY

Homey

Skjold appears as its own device with its own icon, and can be controlled in Flows: start a favourite, switch source or turn the volume down.

SPOTIFY CONNECT

Spotify

Skjold is a Spotify Connect speaker. It requires Spotify Premium.
