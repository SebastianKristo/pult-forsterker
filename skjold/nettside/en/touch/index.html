Skjold Touch

All of Skjold on a 7″ screen

Touch is a network player with a 7″ touchscreen, a Raspberry Pi and its own DAC with RCA and 3.5 mm. Tap the screen to control the player, switch rooms and open the settings. It plays in sync with S1 and Node via Lyrion.

SpecificationsRegister interest ›

7″ touchscreen

800 × 480 with five-point touch.

Its own DAC

PCM5122 with RCA and 3.5 mm.

Synchronised playback

Group Touch with S1 and Node in Lyrion.

Home Assistant · Homey

Touch appears as a Skjold device.

A living screen

Player, home, library, rooms and setup are one tap away. The album art fills the screen while the music plays.

Straight on

The screen frame wraps around the edges of the display, so no circuit board edge shows. The glass sits slightly proud.

Tilted back 40°

The screen rests on a low box that is narrower than the screen, so it seems to float above the table.

All cables at the back

USB-C, RCA, 3.5 mm and Ethernet come out at the back. The volume knob and power button sit on the side.

SOURCES

Sources

AirPlay 2

From iPhone, iPad and Mac. With AirPlay 2, several rooms can play at once.

Bluetooth

From any phone. Start pairing from Setup on the screen.

Spotify Connect

Pick Skjold in the Spotify app. The music streams directly, and you can put your phone away.

Internet radio

Up to 50 favourites via Lyrion, with search across Norwegian stations and the whole world right on the screen.

INTEGRATIONS

Integrations with Lyrion, Home Assistant, Homey and Spotify

LYRION MUSIC SERVER

Lyrion

Skjold shows up as a player in Lyrion, with radio, favourites, local files and synchronised groups across rooms. Skjold connects to an existing Lyrion server, or can run the server itself.

HOME ASSISTANT

Home Assistant

Skjold is discovered automatically as a media player with source, volume and on/off. It can be used in automations, for example radio when you get home.

HOMEY

Homey

Skjold appears as its own device with its own icon, and can be controlled in Flows: start a favourite, switch source or turn the volume down.

SPOTIFY CONNECT

Spotify

Skjold is a Spotify Connect speaker. It requires Spotify Premium.
