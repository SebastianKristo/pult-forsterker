Skjold Touch

All of Skjold on a 7″ screen

Touch is the screen that gathers the house: the music, the lights, the alarm clock and the doorbell. A seven-inch touchscreen, two microphones for voice control and its own audio output for an amplifier or active speakers. It plays in sync with S1 and Node in other rooms.

SpecificationsRegister interest ›

7″ touchscreen

800 × 480 with five-point touch.

Its own audio output

RCA and 3.5 mm for an amplifier or active speakers.

Play in several rooms

Group Touch with S1 and Node, and they play in sync.

Home Assistant · Homey

Touch appears as a Skjold device, with a smart home dashboard on screen.

Voice assistant

Two microphones and its own speaker. Talk to Home Assistant without reaching for your phone.

Alarm clock and night mode

Smart alarm clock, night mode and a doorbell view that shows the camera when someone rings.

Everything is one tap away

Player, smart home, alarm clock, library and rooms. The album art fills the screen while the music plays.

Just screen

A black frame around a 7″ display at 800 × 480. Soft, rounded corners and top edge, and nothing else competing for attention.

Leans back

The screen leans back against the base, so it points up at whoever sits or lies in front of it. It stands steady on the bedside table, with no foot or stand to screw on.

Everything in the base

The base is a rounded, dark grey box printed in PETG. The volume knob sits on the left side and the speaker grille on the right. Power, USB, RCA and 3.5 mm come out at the back, with SKJOLD embossed above.

SOURCES

Play from what you already use

AirPlay 2

Tap the AirPlay icon on iPhone, iPad or Mac and pick Skjold. Pick several rooms and they play together.

Bluetooth

From any phone. Start pairing from Setup on the screen.

Spotify Connect

Pick Skjold in the Spotify app. The music streams directly, and you can put your phone away.

Internet radio

Up to 50 favourites, with search across Norwegian stations and the whole world right on the screen.

HOW IT IS BUILT

How it is built

Everything is drawn, printed and assembled by hand. The base and the screen frame are 3D printed in PETG. The Raspberry Pi, DAC and speaker sit inside the base, and the screen leans against it. All connectors are gathered at the back, so no cables hang out of the sides.

INTEGRATIONS

Fits in with what you already have

LYRION MUSIC SERVER

Lyrion

Skjold shows up as a player in Lyrion, with radio, favourites, local files and synchronised groups across rooms. Skjold connects to an existing Lyrion server, or can run the server itself.

HOME ASSISTANT

Home Assistant

Home Assistant finds Skjold by itself, as a media player with source, volume and power. Use it in automations: radio when you get home, quiet when you go to bed.

HOMEY

Homey

Skjold appears as its own device with its own icon, and can be controlled in Flows: start a favourite, switch source or turn the volume down.

SPOTIFY CONNECT

Spotify

Skjold is a Spotify Connect speaker. It requires Spotify Premium.

Technical specifications

Size
168 × 116 × 88 mm
Enclosure
3D printed in PETG: black screen frame and a rounded, dark grey base with a speaker grille on the side.
Computer
Raspberry Pi 4 (Pi 3 with its own base file)
Display
Waveshare 7″ DSI LCD, 800 × 480, capacitive touch
Audio
Spotpear PCM5122 HiFi DAC HAT, RCA and 3.5 mm
Voice
ReSpeaker Lite (2 microphones, USB) and a 25 × 35 mm speaker, Home Assistant voice satellite
Controls
Touch, B10K volume knob, power button
Power
5 V USB-C
Integrations
Lyrion, Spotify Connect, Home Assistant, Homey
