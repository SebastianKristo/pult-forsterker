Skjold Touch

All of Skjold on a 7″ screen

Touch is a 7″ touchscreen for the home: music player, smart home panel, voice assistant and bedside clock in one. Now in all black, with the base wrapped in dark fabric. It is built on a Raspberry Pi with its own DAC, the enclosure is 3D printed and assembled by hand, and it plays in sync with S1 and Node via Lyrion.

SpecificationsRegister interest ›

7″ touchscreen

800 × 480 with five-point touch.

Its own DAC

PCM5122 with RCA and 3.5 mm.

Synchronised playback

Group Touch with S1 and Node in Lyrion.

Home Assistant · Homey

Touch appears as a Skjold device, with a smart home dashboard on screen.

Voice assistant

ReSpeaker Lite with two microphones and its own speaker. Works as a Home Assistant voice satellite.

Alarm clock and night mode

Smart alarm clock, night mode and a doorbell view that shows the camera when someone rings.

A living screen

Player, home, library, rooms and setup are one tap away. The album art fills the screen while the music plays.

Straight on

A black frame around a 7″ DSI display at 800 × 480. Soft, rounded corners and top edge, and nothing else competing for attention.

More upright

The screen stands 9° more upright than the first version, so it points at whoever sits in front. The rounded back cover is held by magnets and comes off.

Fabric at the back

The base is wrapped in dark fabric, like the back of a Nest Hub, with «SKJOLD TOUCH» embossed on top. Power, RCA and 3.5 mm come out at the back. The volume knob and power button sit on the side.

SOURCES

Sources

AirPlay 2

From iPhone, iPad and Mac. With AirPlay 2, several rooms can play at once.

Bluetooth

From any phone. Start pairing from Setup on the screen.

Spotify Connect

Pick Skjold in the Spotify app. The music streams directly, and you can put your phone away.

Internet radio

Up to 50 favourites via Lyrion, with search across Norwegian stations and the whole world right on the screen.

HOW IT IS BUILT

Screen, base, Pi and knob

Everything is drawn, printed and assembled by hand. The enclosure is 3D printed in PETG, the Pi sits without screws, and the thin, rounded back cover (2.4 mm) is held by four small magnets and comes off. The screen part is about 16 mm thick, and there are no visible side holes for Ethernet or USB.

INTEGRATIONS

Integrations with Lyrion, Home Assistant, Homey and Spotify

LYRION MUSIC SERVER

Lyrion

Skjold shows up as a player in Lyrion, with radio, favourites, local files and synchronised groups across rooms. Skjold connects to an existing Lyrion server, or can run the server itself.

HOME ASSISTANT

Home Assistant

Skjold is discovered automatically as a media player with source, volume and on/off. It can be used in automations, for example radio when you get home.

HOMEY

Homey

Skjold appears as its own device with its own icon, and can be controlled in Flows: start a favourite, switch source or turn the volume down.

SPOTIFY CONNECT

Spotify

Skjold is a Spotify Connect speaker. It requires Spotify Premium.

Technical specifications

Size
168 × 116 × 88 mm
Enclosure
PETG, shown in all black with the base wrapped in dark fabric. Can also be printed with a white screen frame and back cover. Thin, rounded back cover (2.4 mm) with magnets. The screen part is about 16 mm thick.
Computer
Raspberry Pi 4 (Pi 3 with its own base file)
Display
Waveshare 7″ DSI LCD, 800 × 480, capacitive touch
Audio
Spotpear PCM5122 HiFi DAC HAT, RCA and 3.5 mm
Voice
ReSpeaker Lite (2 microphones, USB) and a 25 × 35 mm speaker, Home Assistant voice satellite
Controls
Touch, B10K volume knob, power button
Power
5 V USB-C
Integrations
Lyrion, Spotify Connect, Home Assistant, Homey
