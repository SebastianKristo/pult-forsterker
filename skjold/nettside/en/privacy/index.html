Privacy

Skjold is built to work locally in your home. Most things are never sent anywhere. This page explains what is stored anyway, where, why and how to delete it. It covers the Skjold Lab app (iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch), the Skjold devices, the Skjold-hjem cloud and the website skjold-lab.com.

No trackingNo ads, no analytics and no sharing with third parties for marketing.
Local firstThe app talks to your devices on the local network. No account needed.
You decideThe Skjold-hjem account is optional, and you can delete it yourself at any time.

Who is responsible

The controller is Skjold Lab, Sebastian Kristo Jemtland, Norway. Send privacy questions to jemtlands@proton.me.

The Skjold Lab app

The app has no analytics, advertising or crash reporting SDKs, and it does not track you across apps and websites. Without signing in to Skjold-hjem, nothing about you is sent to Skjold Lab.

Permissions

PermissionUsed for
Local networkFinding and controlling your Skjold devices (Bonjour). Traffic goes directly between the phone and the device.
CameraReading the QR code when you add a device or join a home. Images are not stored or sent anywhere.
MicrophoneRoom correction (EQ): the app listens to the test tone from the speaker and calculates the adjustment on the phone. Audio is not recorded or sent anywhere.
NFCReading and writing Skjold Spole cassettes. The tag holds a reference to music, not personal data.

Services the app contacts

  • Your Skjold devices on the local network.
  • Music servers you add yourself (Navidrome, Plex, Lyrion). Address, username and password are stored only on the phone and sent only to that server.
  • Radio Browser (radio-browser.info) when you search for radio stations, and the station's own server when you play. Searches are sent without anything that identifies you.
  • GitHub to check for new software versions.
  • Skjold-hjem (hjem.skjold-lab.com), only if you sign in.

These services see your IP address, as all online services do. Radio Browser, GitHub and the radio stations have their own privacy policies.

Skjold devices

S1, Node, Touch and the other devices store settings, favourites and listening history locally. Voice control on Skjold devices runs locally or through the Home Assistant, Homey or Lyrion you have set up yourself. The devices contact GitHub for updates. If they are part of a home in Skjold-hjem, they also report name, product, version and local IP address, so the app and website can find them.

A device that is not part of a home can ask to be found by a home on the same network. Its ID and local address are then stored briefly together with a cryptographic hash of the public IP address, not the address itself.

Skjold-hjem (optional account)

Skjold-hjem lets you control your devices remotely, share a home with your family and back up settings. We store:

WhatWhyHow long

Name and profile picture (optional)Shown to other members of your homeUntil you change or delete it
Homes, members and devicesKnowing who has access to which devicesUntil you delete the home or the account

The legal basis is our contract with you (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)) for the account, and our legitimate interest in securing the service (Art. 6(1)(f)) for the security log.

Skjold Cloud

Backups of favourites, routines and settings are encrypted on the device before they are sent (end-to-end). The cloud only sees the part name, version, size and time, never the content. You can delete it at any time (see below).

Shared cassettes and speaker profiles

When you share a cassette, the list is stored encrypted, and the key exists only in the share code. Speaker profiles in Skjold Certified are anonymous. To prevent abuse, only a hash of the IP address is stored, not the address itself.

Who processes the data

Skjold-hjem and the website are hosted by Cloudflare (processor), which also handles sign-in with the email code (Cloudflare Access). Data may be processed outside the EEA. Transfers are covered by the EU Standard Contractual Clauses and the EU–US Data Privacy Framework. We never sell data and do not share it with anyone else.

The website skjold-lab.com

The website uses no tracking cookies and no analytics. Fonts are served from our own server. Cloudflare processes IP addresses and technical logs to deliver the pages and stop attacks.

Delete your data

  • The app: delete the app, and everything it stored on the phone is gone.
  • The account: in your Skjold account (from the app or at hjem.skjold-lab.com) under Data og personvern (Data and privacy) → Slett kontoen (Delete account). The account, profile, passkeys, sessions, memberships and Skjold Cloud data are deleted immediately. If you own a home, transfer or delete it first. Your devices keep working locally.
  • Skjold Cloud only: Data and privacy → delete everything in Skjold Cloud.
  • The security log: events disappear as new ones come in. If you want them deleted right away, send an email.
  • Devices: reset the device from its settings.

Your rights

You have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction, data portability and to object to processing. Email jemtlands@proton.me and we will reply within 30 days. If you believe we process data unlawfully, you can complain to the Norwegian Data Protection Authority, Datatilsynet, or your local authority.

Children

Skjold-hjem is not made for children under 13. Children can use the devices and the app locally, without an account.

Changes

If we change how data is processed, we update this page and the date at the top. For larger changes we will let you know in the app.
