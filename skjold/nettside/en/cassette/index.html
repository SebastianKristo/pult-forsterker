Skjold Kassett

Insert a cassette and it plays

Kassett is a small player with a slot in the front. Each cassette has a tag inside, and you decide what it starts: a radio station, a series, an album or a playlist. Take the cassette out and the music stops. Made for children, and for anyone who misses holding music in their hands.

SpecificationsBuild your own ›

No screen

Insert a cassette to play. Take it out to stop.

The reels turn

They stand still on pause, and wind fast when you change track.

You make the cassettes

Print them yourself, and make labels with the cover art in the app.

Plays in any room

A cassette can start music in the room you choose.

SKJOLD KASSETT

How it works

The cassette holds no music. It only tells Skjold what to play.

1

Print a cassette

Stick an NFC tag in the cassette and put it in the player.

2

Link it in the app

Choose what the cassette starts: radio, album, playlist or a routine.

3

Make the label

The app makes a label with cover art and title for you to print.

4

Insert it

The music starts in the room you chose.

Technical specifications

Size
120 × 62 × 72 mm. The cassette is 50 × 70 × 10 mm
Enclosure
3D printed, with a window in the front
Reader
NFC (an NTAG215 sticker in each cassette)
Reels
Two loose reels per cassette, driven by magnets from a small motor
Computer
ESP32-S3
Power
5 V USB-C
Starts
Radio stations, series, Navidrome albums and playlists, Music Assistant and routines
Requires
A Skjold device on the same network (S1, Node, Touch or Core)
Price
Not for sale. The files are free to download
