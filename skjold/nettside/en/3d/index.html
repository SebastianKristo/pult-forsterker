Good to know

  • 3MF opens in Creality Print or OrcaSlicer with colours and settings. Use STL for other printers.
  • PETG or ASA is recommended (PLA softens in warm and humid rooms). Parts with clips and springs need PETG.
  • Most products are designed and checked by script (collisions, overhangs, fit), but not yet printed and tested. The badge says so.
