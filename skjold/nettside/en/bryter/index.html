Skjold Bryter

Five real switches under the desk

Bryter is a box with five metal toggle switches that hangs under your desk. Each switch is its own on/off switch in the smart home, and it stays where you put it, so you can always see what is on. The mount under the desk is hidden, and the box clicks into place without tools.

STATUS · NOT BUILT YET

Bryter is drawn and checked, but not printed or tested on hardware. The enclosure is checked for collisions, that the box slides into the mount and that nothing needs supports, and the firmware is built for the ESP32 D1 mini and the D1 mini in every release. Follow the work in the build guide on GitHub (Norwegian).

FIVE SWITCHES

Real metal toggle switches

KN3C-101 with a rubber boot. The lever stays where you put it, up is on.

HIDDEN MOUNT

Clicks into place

A flat mount is screwed under the desk. The box slides onto two dovetails and clicks; pull it towards you to take it out.

NO SCREWS

The base clicks in

The base with the back wall clicks in with four clips. The ESP board sits in a holder without screws.

HOME ASSISTANT

Switch 1–5 and events

Five switch sensors, and a “Toggled” event every time a switch flips, for automations that should toggle something.

SAFE

Only 3.3 volts

The switches only carry a weak signal to the ESP, never mains voltage. What switches the power is the smart plugs and lamps you already have.

WIRELESS UPDATES

New firmware with one tap

“Firmware” with an Install button in Home Assistant. No need to take the box down.

THE DESIGN

A clean front, everything else hidden

The front is printed face down, so it comes out completely smooth, with the Skjold mark, SKJOLD and a gold line inlaid in the first layers. The sides are clean: the mount grips two slots in the top of the box, and two spring tongues click onto knobs in the mount when the box is all the way in. The USB cable leaves at the back, under the desk.

HOW IT WORKS

Flip a switch, and something happens at home

Bryter only reports how the switches are set. What should happen is up to you in Home Assistant or Homey.

1

Install the firmware

Connect the ESP board to your computer and press Install at skjold-lab.com/flasher/bryter, before you close the box.

2

Screw up the mount

Four wood screws under the desktop. Slide the box in until it clicks.

3

Connect power

5 V over USB, from a charger or a free USB port. Choose Wi-Fi the first time.

4

Choose what the switches do

Home Assistant finds “Bryter 1” to “Bryter 5”. Link each one to a light, a plug or a scene.

Technical specifications

Status
Drawn and checked. Not built or tested.
Size
182 × 55 × 41 mm, plus a 4 mm mount against the desktop
Switches
5 × KN3C-101 (or E-TEN1021), on/off, M12 thread, with rubber boot
Board
ESP32 D1 mini (MH-ET LIVE MiniKit) or Wemos D1 mini
Wiring
One pin per switch (D1, D2, D5, D6, D7) and a shared GND, no resistors
Mount
Hidden mount with dovetails and a click, 4 wood screws 4 × 16 mm
Power
5 V over USB, cable out the back
Firmware
ESPHome, from the browser, updated over Wi-Fi
Integrations
Home Assistant (Switch 1–5, the Toggled event, diagnostics), Homey through Home Assistant
