Music in every room.

Skjold is amplifiers, players and speakers that play together, controlled from one app. Open-source software. Designed and built in Norway by Skjold Lab.

The family

Find the one that fits the room.

Every device runs the same software, and they all play in sync with each other.

Skjold S1

Streaming amplifier for the desk

A streaming amplifier with AirPlay, Bluetooth, internet radio and two AUX inputs. It has a built-in USB switch for your PC, and everything is controlled with buttons.

Learn more →
Skjold Node

Network player for rooms without an amplifier

Node is a small network player with a Raspberry Pi and a 1.8″ display. It has the same sources and menus as S1, and can play in sync with S1 in other rooms.

Learn more →
Skjold Touch

Player with a 7″ touchscreen

Touch has a 7″ touchscreen, a Raspberry Pi and its own DAC with RCA and 3.5 mm. Control the music and rooms right on the screen.

Learn more →
Skjold Røst

Round speaker with a grille all the way round

Røst is a small active speaker with a 3″ driver, a grille all the way round, and a display, buttons and a gold volume knob on top. It plays in sync with the rest of the house.

Learn more →
Skjold Stav

Remote with a 2.8″ touchscreen

Stav controls Skjold, the TV, Apple TV and Home Assistant from one slim remote, with IR learning and activities like «Watch TV» and «Good night». It charges in Skjold Dokk. A build project, not tested yet.

Learn more →
Skjold Spole

Put in a cassette and the music starts

Spole is a small cassette player without tape. Each cassette has a tag inside, and you decide what it starts. Behind the window, the reels turn while the music plays.

Learn more →
Skjold Brage

Smart devices that talk straight to Home Assistant

Brage is small boxes that talk directly to Home Assistant on your own network. There is no cloud, no account and no separate app. First out: five toggle switches.

Learn more →
Skjold Core

Skjold on a plain Raspberry Pi

Core is Skjold with no case, buttons or display. Connect the Pi to speakers or an amplifier, and control everything from the app.

Learn more →
