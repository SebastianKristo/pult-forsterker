Sound, made in Norway.

Two devices and one software, for the whole house.

Skjold S1
Big sound. Quiet form.
A streaming amplifier with AirPlay, Bluetooth, internet radio and two AUX inputs. It has a built-in USB switch for your PC, and everything is controlled with buttons.
Learn more ›
Skjold Node
Skjold in every room.
Node is a small network player for a room without an amplifier. It has the same sources and menus as S1. It plays in sync with S1 via Lyrion.
Learn more ›
Skjold Core
Skjold on a plain Raspberry Pi.
Got a Raspberry Pi 4 or 3B/3B+ in a drawer? Flash it with Skjold Flasher, connect speakers or an amplifier, and you have AirPlay 2, Spotify Connect, Bluetooth and Lyrion radio. No case, buttons or display – everything is controlled from the app, the browser, Home Assistant or Homey.
