Skjold S1

Big sound. Quiet form.

A streaming amplifier with AirPlay, Bluetooth, internet radio and two AUX inputs. It has a built-in USB switch for your PC, and everything is controlled with buttons.

SpecificationsRegister interest ›
AIRPLAY · BLUETOOTH

One button for both.

Press to switch between AirPlay and Bluetooth. Hold to pair a new phone.

RADIO

Six favourites.

The radio button steps through six Lyrion favourites. Hold it to play the favourite you have chosen.

USB

PC audio in one press.

A built-in USB switch moves your USB devices between Skjold and your PC.

AMPLIFIER

2 × 50 W class D.

TPA3116 at 24 V with an I²S DAC. The headphone output is at the front.

One surface.

The display sits behind smoked acrylic, flush with the front. You only see what is lit.

Folded, not bolted.

The enclosure is one piece of 1.5 mm aluminium with R9 corners. The seams are underneath.

Everything within reach.

The front plate lifts out. Raspberry Pi, DAC and amplifier sit on separate levels.

Connections at the back.

M8 speaker posts, AUX 1 and 2, three USB-C, LAN, 24 V and a main switch.

SOURCES

All your music. One place.

AirPlay 2

From iPhone, iPad and Mac. With AirPlay 2, several rooms can play at once.

Bluetooth

From any phone. Hold AIR / BT to pair a new phone.

Spotify Connect

Pick Skjold in the Spotify app. The music streams directly, and you can put your phone away.

Internet radio

Six favourites via Lyrion, one for each press of the radio button.

AUX 1 · AUX 2

3.5 mm inputs at the back, for a turntable, TV or another source. S1 only.

USB audio from PC

Skjold becomes your PC’s sound card with one press of USB. S1 only.

USB SWITCH

One button. Two computers.

S1 has a built-in USB switch. Keyboard, mouse and anything else you plug into the three USB-C ports at the back follow along when you press USB, either to Skjold or to your PC. When the PC is selected, Skjold is its sound card.

INTEGRATIONS

Works with what you have.

LYRION MUSIC SERVER

Lyrion

The heart of the system. Skjold appears as a player, with radio, favourites, local files and synchronised groups across rooms.

HOME ASSISTANT

Home Assistant

Skjold is discovered automatically as a media player with source, volume and on/off. It can be used in automations, for example radio when you get home.

HOMEY

Homey

Skjold appears as its own device with its own icon, and can be controlled in Flows: start a favourite, switch source or turn the volume down.

SPOTIFY CONNECT

Spotify

Skjold is a Spotify Connect speaker. It requires Spotify Premium.
