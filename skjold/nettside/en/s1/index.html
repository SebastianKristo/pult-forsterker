Skjold S1

Streaming amplifier for the desk

A streaming amplifier with AirPlay, Bluetooth, internet radio and two AUX inputs. It has a wide 4.58″ colour display, a USB switch for two PCs, and everything is controlled with buttons.

SpecificationsRegister interest ›
AIRPLAY · BLUETOOTH

AirPlay and Bluetooth on one button

Press to switch between AirPlay and Bluetooth. Hold to pair a new phone.

RADIO

Six radio favourites

The radio button steps through six favourites. Hold for 3 seconds to save the station that is playing.

USB

Two PCs on one button

Press to select USB, press again to switch between PC 1 and PC 2. Keyboard, mouse and audio follow along.

AMPLIFIER

2 × 50 W

Enough for a pair of bookshelf speakers on the desk. Headphone output at the front, and the speakers switch off when you plug in.

NIGHT MODE

Double-press for night

Double-press the power button to start night mode in the house or the room via Home Assistant or Homey. The display shows the automation while it runs.

STANDBY

16 standby screens

Clocks, VU meters, radio dial, weather and calendar from Home Assistant. Choose on the device, in the app or on the web.

THE DISPLAY

4.58″ colour display, 960 × 320

One surface

The display is a 4.58″ strip behind smoked acrylic in a champagne aluminium front plate. You only see what is lit.

Bent, not bolted

The enclosure is bent from a single sheet of aluminium, with rounded edges along the top and bottom and the seams underneath. The front is milled from 3 mm aluminium, with engraved text.

Connections at the back

Speaker posts L/R, AUX 1 and 2, USB to PC 1 and PC 2, LAN, 24 V in and a rocker switch. Four USB-A ports sit on the left side.

SOURCES

Sources

AirPlay 2

From iPhone, iPad and Mac. With AirPlay 2, several rooms can play at once.

Bluetooth

From any phone. Hold AIR / BT to pair a new phone.

Spotify Connect

Pick Skjold in the Spotify app. The music streams directly, and you can put your phone away.

Internet radio

Six favourites, one for each press of the radio button.

AUX 1 · AUX 2

3.5 mm inputs at the back, for a turntable, TV or another source. S1 only.

USB audio from PC

Skjold becomes your PC’s sound card with one press of USB. S1 only.

USB SWITCH

Share keyboard and mouse with your PC

S1 has a built-in USB switch. Keyboard, mouse and anything else you plug into the four USB-A ports on the left side follow along when you press USB: to PC 1 or PC 2. Two built-in USB sound cards make Skjold the sound card for both PCs.

INTEGRATIONS

Integrations with Lyrion, Home Assistant, Homey and Spotify

LYRION MUSIC SERVER

Lyrion

Skjold shows up as a player in Lyrion, with radio, favourites, local files and synchronised groups across rooms. Skjold connects to an existing Lyrion server, or can run the server itself.

HOME ASSISTANT

Home Assistant

Skjold is discovered automatically as a media player with source, volume and on/off. It can be used in automations, for example radio when you get home.

HOMEY

Homey

Skjold appears as its own device with its own icon, and can be controlled in Flows: start a favourite, switch source or turn the volume down.

SPOTIFY CONNECT

Spotify

Skjold is a Spotify Connect speaker. It requires Spotify Premium.

Technical specifications

Size
224 × 183 × 50 mm (case 224 × 180 × 49.6 + 3 mm front plate)
Enclosure
Aluminium 1.5 mm, R9 along top and bottom, black, bent sheet metal. The seams are underneath.
Front
CNC-milled aluminium 3 mm, champagne, laser-engraved text. Smoked acrylic display window.
Amplifier
TPA3116D2 class D, 2 × 50 W @ 4 Ω, 24 V
DAC
PCM5102 I²S DAC
Display
4.58″ colour display, 960 × 320, wide strip
Computer
Raspberry Pi 4
Inputs
AUX 1, AUX 2 (3.5 mm), USB audio from PC 1 and PC 2, LAN, Wi-Fi
Outputs
Speaker posts L/R, 3.5 mm headphones at the front (speakers mute when plugged in)
USB
2 built-in USB sound cards (PC 1 and PC 2) and a USB switch with 4 USB-A on the left side
Power
24 V in, rocker switch at the back
Controls
Volume wheel with press, 4 metal buttons with white LED ring (BT/AirPlay, AUX, RADIO, USB), power button with LED ring
