Skjold Røst

Small speaker for bathroom, kitchen and kids’ room

Røst is a small active speaker with a 3″ full-range driver in a bass-reflex cabinet, a 2.42″ display and buttons for power and four radio favourites on top. It plays in sync with S1, Node and Touch via Lyrion, and you print it on your own 3D printer.

SpecificationsRegister interest ›

112 × 124.5 × 142 mm

Fits on a shelf or next to the sink.

3″ full-range, 15 W

Dayton Audio RS75-4 in bass reflex, class D amplifier.

Display and favourites

2.42″ OLED and buttons for power and radio favourite 1–4.

Handles steam

Gaskets in the seams and a rear port. Not waterproof.

Synchronised playback

Røst is a player in Lyrion and groups with the rest.

Print it yourself

Ready plates for Creality K2, no supports.

Everything on top

The display and buttons sit on top in front of the volume wheel. The top is flat. Raised rims around the display window and the buttons and a sealed collar under the volume wheel keep splashes out.

Display and favourites

The 2.42″ OLED shows station, track, artist and volume. Four buttons start your radio favourites, and a long press saves the station that is playing.

Tight seam

Front and body are made from the same outer shape, with a TPU gasket in the seam. The grille is a separate part that presses on.

Port and connectors at the back

The bass port faces backwards, so water does not run in. The 12 V jack is at the back, and the adapter can stay outside the bathroom.

HOW IT IS BUILT

Printed, sealed and screwed together

The enclosure is printed in PETG or ASA on an ordinary 3D printer, with TPU gaskets. The parts are screwed together with M3 heat-set inserts. The electronics sit in their own compartment below the sound chamber.

IN THE BATHROOM

Handles steam, not the shower

The seams are sealed with TPU, the bass port faces backwards, and there is water-repellent cloth behind the grille. The buttons on top are ordinary printed caps and are not sealed, so the top only handles a little splashing. Do not put Røst in the shower, and keep the power adapter outside the bathroom. Røst itself runs on safe 12 V.

INTEGRATIONS

Integrations with Lyrion, Home Assistant and Homey

LYRION MUSIC SERVER

Lyrion

Skjold shows up as a player in Lyrion, with radio, favourites, local files and synchronised groups across rooms. Skjold connects to an existing Lyrion server, or can run the server itself.

HOME ASSISTANT

Home Assistant

Skjold is discovered automatically as a media player with source, volume and on/off. It can be used in automations, for example radio when you get home.

HOMEY

Homey

Skjold appears as its own device with its own icon, and can be controlled in Flows: start a favourite, switch source or turn the volume down.

Technical specifications

Size
112 × 124.5 × 142 mm (W × D × H, with grille and feet). The volume wheel sticks up 14.6 mm.
Enclosure
3D printed in PETG or ASA, TPU gaskets in the seams, removable grille, SKJOLD logo in its own colour. Ready plates for Creality K2.
Driver
3″ full-range, 4 Ω (Dayton Audio RS75-4 recommended)
Cabinet
Bass reflex, about 0.97 l net, rear port, calculated tuning about 83 Hz
Amplifier
TPA3118D2 in mono (PBTL), about 15 W into 4 Ω at 12 V
DAC
PCM5102A, I²S
Computer
ESP32-S3 N16R8 (Wi-Fi)
Display
2.42″ OLED 128 × 64, white on black
Controls
Volume wheel with press, power and radio favourite 1–4 on top
Power
12 V / 2 A adapter
Player
Squeezebox player in Lyrion (squeezelite-esp32), in sync with S1, Node and Touch
