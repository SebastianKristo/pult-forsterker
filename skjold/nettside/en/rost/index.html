Skjold Røst

Round speaker with a grille all the way round

Røst is a small active speaker with a 3″ full-range driver in a bass-reflex cabinet and a grille of hexagonal holes all the way round. On top there is a 2.42″ display, five buttons and a gold volume knob. It plays in sync with S1, Node and Touch via Lyrion, and you print it on your own 3D printer.

108 × 108 × 150 mm

Rounded, fits on a shelf, a table or a bedside table.

3″ full-range, 15 W

Dayton Audio RS75-4 in bass reflex, class D amplifier.

Display and favourites

2.42″ OLED, on/off and radio favourite 1–4.

Grille all the way round

2,667 hexagonal holes, with SKJOLD in gold on the front.

Synchronised playback

Røst is a player in Lyrion and groups with the rest.

Print it yourself

Ready plates for Creality K2, no supports and no visible screws.

Everything on top

Five buttons at the front, the display behind a clear window flush with the top, and the gold volume knob at the back. The lid has a soft, rounded edge.

Display and favourites

The 2.42″ OLED shows station, track, artist and volume. Four buttons start your radio favourites, and a long press saves the station that is playing.

Grille all the way round

The grille sleeve has Skjold’s hexagon pattern all the way round, with a dark core behind it that gives the grille depth. SKJOLD sits in gold at the bottom of the front.

Connectors at the back, port underneath

12 V and USB-C are at the bottom of the back. The bass port goes down through the base, and the rubber feet give it room to breathe.

HOW IT IS BUILT

Printed and held together by four rods

The core, the grille sleeve, the lid and the base are printed in PETG or PLA on an ordinary 3D printer, without supports. Four M3 threaded rods run from the lid through the core to the base, so no screws are visible. The driver sits on a replaceable plate, so you can switch to another 3″ driver by printing a new plate.

WHERE IT FITS

For the living room, kitchen and bedroom

The grille is open all the way round, so Røst does not handle splashes or steam. Put it on a shelf, a table or a bedside table. It runs on safe 12 V from an ordinary adapter.

INTEGRATIONS

Integrations with Lyrion, Home Assistant and Homey

LYRION MUSIC SERVER

Lyrion

Skjold shows up as a player in Lyrion, with radio, favourites, local files and synchronised groups across rooms. Skjold connects to an existing Lyrion server, or can run the server itself.

HOME ASSISTANT

Home Assistant

Skjold is discovered automatically as a media player with source, volume and on/off. It can be used in automations, for example radio when you get home.

HOMEY

Homey

Skjold appears as its own device with its own icon, and can be controlled in Flows: start a favourite, switch source or turn the volume down.

Technical specifications

Size
108 × 108 × 150 mm (W × D × H), plus 6 mm rubber feet. The volume knob stands about 14 mm above the top.
Enclosure
3D printed in PETG or PLA: core, grille sleeve with 2,667 hexagonal holes, lid and base, held together by four M3 threaded rods. SKJOLD logo and volume knob in gold. Ready plates for Creality K2.
Driver
3″ full-range, 4 Ω (Dayton Audio RS75-4 recommended), on a replaceable driver plate
Cabinet
Bass reflex, about 1.1 l net, Ø18 × 110 mm port down through the base, calculated tuning about 74 Hz
Amplifier
TPA3118D2 in mono (PBTL), about 15 W into 4 Ω at 12 V
DAC
PCM5102A, I²S
Computer
ESP32-S3 N16R8 (Wi-Fi)
Display
2.42″ OLED 128 × 64, white text on black
Controls
Volume knob (EC11) with push, on/off and radio favourite 1–4 on top
Power
12 V / 2 A adapter
Player
Squeezebox player in Lyrion (squeezelite-esp32), in sync with S1, Node and Touch
