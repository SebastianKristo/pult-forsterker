Support for more devices

Skjold is built for Raspberry Pi 4 and 3B/3B+ today. The devices below can get support if there is interest. Send an email and tell me what you have.

Raspberry Pi 5 and Zero 2 W

With a sound card, USB DAC or HDMI.

Sound cards for Raspberry Pi

DAC and amplifier boards, for example HiFiBerry and IQaudio.

Old PC or Intel Mac

With Linux: full Skjold with AirPlay 2, Spotify and Lyrion.

Windows and macOS

As a separate app with Spotify and Lyrion.
