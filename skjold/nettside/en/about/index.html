THE IDEA

Why Skjold?

Skjold started with a problem nobody had solved for me. I wanted an amplifier that talked to my smart home, and I wanted the clutter off my desk. If I couldn’t find it, I would have to make it myself.

Along the way I also built in a smart USB switch, so the PC and the amplifier share what’s on the desk. Then I talked to people around me, and several of them had the same problem.

That’s when it became more than a 3D print and a bit of soldering. It became something that matters, and that should feel finished. That is Skjold.

DEVELOPMENT

From desk amplifier to two products.

  1. 01

    Desk amplifier

    The first version was a printed amplifier for the desk, with a Raspberry Pi and a TPA3116.

  2. 02

    USB switch and display

    v2 got a built-in USB switch for the PC and the choice between OLED and TFT behind smoked acrylic.

  3. 03

    From plastic to metal

    The enclosure was drawn for both CNC and sheet metal. The choice fell on folded aluminium with R9 corners and the seams underneath.

  4. 04

    The name Skjold

    The product got a name and a logo: five bars like a sound image.

  5. 05

    Skjold OS

    Menus for radio, AirPlay and Bluetooth, per-source equaliser, standby clocks, app and web interface.

  6. 06

    Skjold Node

    A small printed player for the rest of the house, with the same software.

  7. 07

    Into production

    The first enclosures, SKJ-S1-0001 and 0002, were ordered from PCBWay and JLC.
