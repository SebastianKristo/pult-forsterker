Skjold Sensor

Knows how the room is doing

Sensor is a small room sensor for the wall, ceiling or a shelf. It measures temperature and humidity, and can also measure light and see motion. It works out “feels like”, comfort, presence and whether it is dark by itself, and shows up in Home Assistant, Homey and Skjold on its own.

Install the firmware ›Build guide ›
STATUS · NOT BUILT YET

Sensor is drawn and checked, but not printed or tested on hardware. The enclosure is checked for collisions and overhangs, and the firmware is built for ESP32 and D1 mini in every release. Follow the work in the build guide on GitHub (Norwegian).

TEMPERATURE · HUMIDITY

Always measuring

A DHT11 module by default, SHT40 as an upgrade. The air around the sensor is separated from the ESP's heat by a double wall.

LIGHT

Lux, and whether it is dark

A BH1750 behind a small window in the front gives the brightness in lux, a light level in words and “Dark” on/off.

MOTION

Motion and presence

The AM312 dome sees motion. The sensor keeps “Presence” on for a while after the last motion, so the lights stay on while you sit still.

CALCULATED

Feels like and comfort

Feels like, dew point, absolute humidity, comfort (cold, pleasant, warm, humid) and “High humidity” for bathrooms and basements.

SETTINGS

From Homey and Home Assistant

Update interval, how long motion stays on and whether the status LED may light, without flashing again.

WIRELESS UPDATES

New firmware with one tap

“Firmware” with an Install button in Home Assistant, and Update firmware under Maintenance in Homey.

THE DESIGN

A small box with its own air chamber

The sensor sits at the bottom in its own vented chamber, separated from the ESP board by a double wall with an air gap, so the board's heat does not affect the reading. The lid has the PIR dome, a light window and a gold line, and is held by two screws from the back. It hangs on the wall from two keyholes, or stands on a desk stand.

VARIANTS

Pick the board you have

The enclosure exists for the ESP32-WROOM DevKit, the Wemos D1 mini and the ESP32 D1 mini. On the ESP32 D1 mini and the D1 mini with USB-C, the cable leaves at the back, at the bottom, straight down the wall. The lid comes with or without the PIR and light window, and the humidity sensor can be a DHT11 module or a small SHT40 board.

SETTING IT UP

From USB to smart home in five minutes

The firmware is ESPHome and installs from the browser. You do not have to build anything yourself.

1

Install the firmware

Connect the board to your computer and press Install at skjold-lab.com/flasher/sensor (Chrome or Edge).

2

Choose Wi-Fi

Right after flashing, or from your phone through the “Skjold Sensor” network.

3

It shows up

Home Assistant finds it with ESPHome, the Skjold integration shows it as a Skjold device, and in Homey you add it under Skjold.

4

Use the values

Lights that turn on with motion in the dark, an alert on high humidity, or just the temperature on Touch.

HOME ASSISTANT · HOMEY · SKJOLD

The values where you need them

In Home Assistant, Sensor arrives both through ESPHome and as its own Skjold Sensor device in the Skjold integration, with temperature, humidity, light, motion, presence, comfort and diagnostics (IP, Wi-Fi, uptime). In Homey you get the same values, flow cards for presence and darkness, and the settings on the device.

Technical specifications

Status
Drawn and checked. Not built or tested.
Size
About 54–70 × 66–76 × 28–30 mm, depending on board and sensor
Board
ESP32-WROOM DevKit, Wemos D1 mini (ESP8266) or ESP32 D1 mini
Temperature and humidity
DHT11 module (default) or SHT40 / SHT31 / AHT20
Light
BH1750 (GY-302), optional
Motion
AM312 mini PIR, optional (HC-SR501 or LD2410 also work)
Calculated
Feels like, dew point, absolute humidity, comfort, light level, dark, high humidity, presence, motion count
Power
5 V over USB
Firmware
ESPHome, from the browser, updated over Wi-Fi
Integrations
Home Assistant, Homey, Skjold
