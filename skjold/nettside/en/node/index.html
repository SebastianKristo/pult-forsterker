Skjold Node

Network player for rooms without an amplifier

Node is a small network player with a Raspberry Pi and a 1.8″ display. It has the same sources and menus as S1, and can play in sync with S1 in other rooms.

SpecificationsRegister interest ›

91 × 74 × 45 mm

Fits on a bookshelf. The lid tilts 14° towards you.

1.8″ colour display

The same interface as S1.

Play in several rooms

Group Node and S1, and they play in sync.

Home Assistant · Homey

Node appears as a Skjold device.

No screws

The lid clicks on, and the Pi is held by spring clips with a locking wedge.

Lit buttons

Each of the four buttons has its own LED.

Everything on top

Four tactile buttons with LEDs, the display and the volume knob sit on a lid that tilts towards you. The labels are printed into the lid.

Two colours, one print

The lid is printed in PLA and the base in PETG, each in its own colour, with the text in a third colour in the first layers. Choose from 8 colour combinations.

Raspberry Pi inside

The Pi clicks into the base and is held by two spring clips with a locking wedge. The lid clicks on with four tabs. There are no screws in the whole enclosure.

Openings for every port

The base comes for Pi 4 and Pi 3. Each has openings only where the ports are, and a wide slot for micro-SD. The front panel with USB-C, HDMI and audio slides out.

SOURCES

Sources

AirPlay 2

From iPhone, iPad and Mac. With AirPlay 2, several rooms can play at once.

Bluetooth

From any phone. Hold AIR / BT to pair a new phone.

Spotify Connect

Pick Skjold in the Spotify app. The music streams directly, and you can put your phone away.

Internet radio

Six favourites, one for each press of the radio button.

INTEGRATIONS

Integrations with Lyrion, Home Assistant, Homey and Spotify

LYRION MUSIC SERVER

Lyrion

Skjold shows up as a player in Lyrion, with radio, favourites, local files and synchronised groups across rooms. Skjold connects to an existing Lyrion server, or can run the server itself.

HOME ASSISTANT

Home Assistant

Skjold is discovered automatically as a media player with source, volume and on/off. It can be used in automations, for example radio when you get home.

HOMEY

Homey

Skjold appears as its own device with its own icon, and can be controlled in Flows: start a favourite, switch source or turn the volume down.

SPOTIFY CONNECT

Spotify

Skjold is a Spotify Connect speaker. It requires Spotify Premium.

Technical specifications

Size
91 × 74 × 45–61 mm (lid tilts 14°: 45 mm at the front, about 61 mm at the back)
Enclosure
3D printed: PLA lid, PETG base, text in a third colour. No screws: the lid clicks on, the Pi is held by spring clips. 8 colour combinations.
Computer
Raspberry Pi 4 / 3B / 3B+
Display
1.8″ colour display ST7735S, 128 × 160, clips on without screws
Controls
4 tactile buttons with printed caps and their own LED (RADIO, AIR/BT, ◀◀, ▶▶). Volume: B10K potentiometer with ADS1115.
Ports
USB, Ethernet, HDMI, 3.5 mm, micro-SD
Power
5 V USB-C
Integrations
Lyrion, Spotify Connect, Home Assistant, Homey
