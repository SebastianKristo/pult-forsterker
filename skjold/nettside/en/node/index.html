Skjold Node

Network player for rooms without an amplifier

Node is a small network player with a Raspberry Pi and a 1.8″ display. It has the same sources and menus as S1, and can play in sync with S1 via Lyrion.

SpecificationsRegister interest ›

91 × 74 × 34 mm

Fits on a bookshelf.

1.8″ colour display

The same interface as S1.

Synchronised playback

Group Node and S1 in Lyrion.

Home Assistant · Homey

Node appears as a Skjold device.

Controls on top

The display, the volume knob and four metal buttons sit on top. The labels are printed into the lid.

Printed in two colours

Lid and base are printed separately, each in its own colour. The logo sits in the first layers.

Raspberry Pi inside

Four screws from below hold both the Pi and the lid. No screws are visible from above.

Openings for every port

The base comes for Pi 4 and Pi 3. Each has openings only where the ports are, and a wide slot for micro-SD.

SOURCES

Sources

AirPlay 2

From iPhone, iPad and Mac. With AirPlay 2, several rooms can play at once.

Bluetooth

From any phone. Hold AIR / BT to pair a new phone.

Spotify Connect

Pick Skjold in the Spotify app. The music streams directly, and you can put your phone away.

Internet radio

Six favourites via Lyrion, one for each press of the radio button.

INTEGRATIONS

Integrations with Lyrion, Home Assistant, Homey and Spotify

LYRION MUSIC SERVER

Lyrion

Skjold shows up as a player in Lyrion, with radio, favourites, local files and synchronised groups across rooms.

HOME ASSISTANT

Home Assistant

Skjold is discovered automatically as a media player with source, volume and on/off. It can be used in automations, for example radio when you get home.

HOMEY

Homey

Skjold appears as its own device with its own icon, and can be controlled in Flows: start a favourite, switch source or turn the volume down.

SPOTIFY CONNECT

Spotify

Skjold is a Spotify Connect speaker. It requires Spotify Premium.
