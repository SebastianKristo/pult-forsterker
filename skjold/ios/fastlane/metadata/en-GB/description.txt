Skjold Lab is the app for Skjold – amplifiers and streamers you can build yourself, with open-source software. The app talks directly to your devices on the local network. No account is needed, and nothing is sent to a cloud unless you want it to be.

The app is currently in Norwegian.

CONTROL YOUR MUSIC
• Play, pause, skip and set the volume on S1, Node and Touch
• See what's playing, with artwork, in every room
• Internet radio from around the world, with search and favourites
• Your own music library from Navidrome, Plex or Lyrion
• Popular tracks, albums and artists in your library

SKJOLD SPOLE
• Make NFC cassettes: one tag for an album, a playlist or a radio station
• Put the cassette on the player and the music starts
• Print labels and share cassettes with others

SOUND THAT FITS THE ROOM
• Room correction: your phone listens to a test tone and tunes the EQ to your room
• Speaker profiles and manual EQ

AT HOME AND AWAY
• Add new devices with a QR code
• Skjold-hjem (optional): control your devices remotely and share your home with family
• Skjold Cloud: end-to-end encrypted backup of favourites and settings
• Home Screen widgets and an Apple Watch app

PRIVACY
No ads, no tracking and no analytics. Camera, microphone and NFC are used only locally on your phone.

Requires a Skjold device (S1, Node, Touch or Skjold Core on a Raspberry Pi). Without a device you can explore the app in demo mode. Learn more and build your own at skjold-lab.com.
